Distress Signals

Learn the gestures that will trigger emergency alerts:

✋

Open Hand

Hold your hand open with fingers spread to signal for help

✊

Closed Fist

Make a fist to signal an emergency situation

How to Use Distress Signals

  1. Make sure you're in a well-lit area
  2. Position your hand clearly in front of the camera
  3. Hold the gesture for at least 2 seconds
  4. Wait for the confirmation message

Note: When a distress signal is detected, the app will automatically send your location to your emergency contacts.
