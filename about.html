About Silent SOS

Our Mission

Silent SOS is a revolutionary safety application that allows users to send emergency alerts using hand gestures. Our mission is to provide a discreet and effective way for people to call for help in dangerous situations.

How It Works

The app uses advanced gesture recognition technology to detect specific hand movements. When a distress gesture is recognized, the app automatically sends an alert to your emergency contacts with your location information.

Key Features

  • Gesture-based emergency alerts
  • Automatic location sharing
  • Emergency contact management
  • Discreet operation
  • Real-time alert system

Safety First

Your safety is our top priority. All data is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your information with third parties without your explicit consent.
